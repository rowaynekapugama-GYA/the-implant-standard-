Implant dentistry near Elsternwick

Why Elsternwick patients choose us.

Implant treatment is only as good as its planning. Every case at The Implant Standard begins with a free consultation that includes intraoral scans and CBCT 3D imaging — mapping your bone, nerves and bite before anything is decided. Your implant position is planned digitally first, then placed precisely.

Treatment is led by Dr Hyun Soo Yu, who holds a Master of Science in Implantology and Fellowship of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI), supported by a team focused on implant and restorative dentistry across our St Kilda and Parkdale clinics.

Whether you're missing one tooth, several, or need a full-arch All-on-4 solution, you'll get honest options, a fixed itemised quote, and every payment pathway explained — including interest-free plans and early release of super* for eligible patients.

Treatments for Elsternwick patients

  • Single tooth implants — replace one missing tooth permanently, $4,500–$6,500.
  • All-on-4 full arch — a full set of fixed teeth on 4–6 implants, often same-day.
  • Implant-supported dentures — lock loose dentures in place.
  • Crowns & bridges — restore damaged or missing teeth.
  • Sleep dentistry — sedation and GA options if you'd rather sleep through it.

How much do dental implants cost near Elsternwick?
A single dental implant at The Implant Standard typically ranges from $4,500 to $6,500 including the implant, abutment and crown. Full-arch All-on-4 treatment is quoted individually after 3D imaging. Every Elsternwick patient receives a fixed, itemised quote at their free consultation — with interest-free payment plans, HICAPS claiming and early release of super* available for eligible patients.
