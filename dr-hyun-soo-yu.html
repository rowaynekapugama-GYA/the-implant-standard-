About

Meet Dr Hyun.

Dr Hyun Soo Yu graduated with honours in Dental Science from the University of Melbourne in 2006, and spent his early career in Sydney refining his craft across a busy mix of surgical and restorative dentistry before returning to Melbourne.

Since 2012 he has led Grey Street Dentist in St Kilda, and today also leads Synergy Dental Group in Parkdale — the two clinics behind The Implant Standard. His postgraduate journey has been built deliberately around one discipline: replacing missing teeth to the highest standard. That path includes a Master of Science in Implantology, Fellowship of the International Congress of Oral Implantologists (FICOI), and postgraduate orthodontic training that informs complete, whole-smile planning.

Hyun's approach is defined by digital precision — CBCT 3D imaging, digital smile design and guided surgery — paired with an unhurried, honest consultation style. Patients consistently describe the same experience: everything explained clearly, every option on the table, and no pressure to proceed.

Away from the clinic, Hyun is a dedicated family man who brings the same calm consistency to his patients that he brings to everything else he does.

Areas of focus

  • Implant dentistry from single teeth to complex multi-implant cases.
  • All-on-4 full-arch rehabilitation the flagship treatment of The Implant Standard.
  • Full-mouth reconstruction staged rehabilitation of worn and failing dentitions.
  • Digital planning & guided surgery CBCT imaging and digitally designed outcomes.
  • Complete smile planning implantology informed by postgraduate orthodontic training.
