Surgical and invasive procedures

Any surgical or invasive procedure carries risks. Before proceeding, you should seek a second opinion from an appropriately qualified health practitioner.

Dental implant treatment, All-on-4 full-arch treatment, extractions, bone grafting and sedation all carry risks and potential complications, which will be explained to you during your consultation as part of informed consent.

Treatment outcomes and imagery

Before and after images on this website show actual treatment results achieved for patients of Grey Street Dentist and Synergy Dental Group. Results vary between individuals and may not be typical. Treatment outcomes depend on each patient's oral health, clinical suitability, treatment needs and compliance with dental advice.

No guarantee is made that any result shown can be achieved for you. A consultation and clinical assessment — including appropriate imaging — are required before any treatment plan, timeframe, cost or expected outcome can be confirmed.

Suitability and eligibility

Statements on this website about candidacy (for example, for dental implants or All-on-4) are general in nature. Your suitability can only be determined by clinical assessment. Indicative pricing shown is a guide only; you will receive a personalised, itemised quote before treatment begins.

Superannuation information

References to early release of superannuation describe an Australian Taxation Office (ATO) process for compassionate release of super for eligible medical and dental treatment. Approval is made by the ATO, not by our practices, and is subject to eligibility criteria. Information on this website about superannuation is general only and is not financial advice. Consider obtaining independent financial advice before accessing your superannuation, as early release may affect your retirement savings and may have tax implications.
