About

Meet Dr David.

Dr David Kim completed his Doctor of Dental Surgery at the University of Melbourne and holds registration to practise dentistry in both Australia and Canada.

After graduating, David spent four years practising in Cairns, Queensland — an experience that exposed him to an exceptionally broad range of restorative and general dentistry, and shaped a practical, patient-first approach to care. He has since relocated back to Melbourne and provides care across our locations.

David's focus is quality restorative dentistry: crowns, bridges and implant restorations executed carefully and built to last. Patients appreciate his straightforward explanations and thorough, unrushed appointments.

Areas of focus

  • Restorative dentistry crowns, bridges and implant restorations.
  • General & preventive care keeping implant patients healthy long-term.
  • Broad clinical experience four years of wide-ranging practice in regional Queensland.
  • Clear communication straightforward options, honestly explained.
