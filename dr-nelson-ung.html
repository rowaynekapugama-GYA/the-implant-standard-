About

Meet Dr Nelson.

Dr Nelson Ung completed a Bachelor of Biomedicine with honours before graduating with his Doctor of Dental Surgery from the University of Melbourne in 2018.

Nelson has a special interest in dental implant surgery, and has dedicated considerable time to advanced education and hands-on surgical training in the field. He pairs that surgical focus with a second interest in clear aligner orthodontics — valuable in implant dentistry, where tooth position and spacing often shape the ideal result.

Patients know Nelson for a meticulous, gentle style and a knack for putting nervous patients at ease — taking time to explain each step so nothing feels rushed or unexpected.

Outside dentistry, Nelson loves travel, sport and time with family and friends — and brings that easy-going energy into the surgery with him.

Areas of focus

  • Implant surgery special interest with ongoing advanced surgical training.
  • Clear aligner orthodontics alignment that supports better implant and restorative outcomes.
  • Restorative dentistry crowns, bridges and implant restorations.
  • Anxious patients a calm, unhurried chairside manner.
